Messages For You 💌

TWENTY! 🥹🤍

It feels so beautiful to witness you reach this milestone. I pray that this new chapter brings you closer to everything that is good for you. I pray that your twenties are filled with beautiful discoveries, meaningful growth, beneficial knowledge, answered prayers, sincere friendships, good health, contentment, and opportunities that bring you closer to the life you dream of living.

But above everything else, I pray that these years bring you closer to Allah Azza wa Jal. May your relationship with Him become stronger, your heart become more attached to His remembrance, your salah become a source of peace, and your faith become your greatest strength through every season of life. May Allah grant you the wisdom to recognise what is good for you, the courage to walk away from what is not, and the patience to trust His plans even when you cannot immediately understand them.

May Allah open every door of khayr for you. Every door that contains goodness, ease, success, peace, happiness, beneficial opportunities, and beautiful blessings, may He open it for you in the most perfect way. And every door that may appear beautiful but holds harm for you, may Allah lovingly turn you away from it and replace it with something far better.

And whenever life feels uncertain, may you always remember that the same Allah who brought us together is capable of writing the most beautiful chapters of your life. Never lose hope in His mercy, my love. Never underestimate the power of your du'as, and never forget that what Allah has written for you will reach you at the time He knows is best.

My prayer for you will never be limited to your birthday. I will pray for you on ordinary days, on difficult days, in moments of happiness, and in moments when I may not even know what you are going through. I will continue to ask Allah to protect you, guide you, bless you, and grant you the very best in everything that is good for you.

And if there is one thing I wish for our friendship, it is that we continue to grow together in goodness. May we witness each other's beautiful milestones. May we celebrate each other's successes without envy, comfort each other through our difficult seasons, correct each other with love, and remain sincere in our intentions towards one another.

my princess, I pray for something even more beautiful than all of this.

I want a friendship with you that does not end with this dunya, Ummi. I want us to be among those who are reunited by the mercy of Allah in Jannatul Firdaus, smiling at the fact that He allowed us to find each other here and blessed us with something even more beautiful in the akhirah.

I love you so much, my princess. More than I can properly explain, more than this letter could ever contain, and more than you may fully realise.

You are my answered du'a, my sister in Deen, my favourite confidante, my forever princess, and one of the most beautiful blessings Allah has written into my life.

And when we are old, in sha Allah, I hope we can look back at these days, laugh about how we met, remember BLUSH FOR THE AKHIRAH, and thank Allah for allowing us to be part of each other's stories.

Until then, I will keep making du'a for you, celebrating you, correcting you when necessary, learning from you, and loving you for the sake of Allah.

Happy birthday, my twenty-year-old princess! May Allah make your life beautiful in ways that bring you closer to Him, and may He grant you a place in Jannatul Firdaus where we will, bi idhnillah, be reunited forever.

I love you endlessly, Ummi. May Allah bless you today, tomorrow, and for all the beautiful years ahead.

Forever your girl, forever your friend, and forever grateful that Allah allowed me to meet you. 🤍🎀

Barakallahu fi umrik, my princess. May Allah place barakah in your life, increase you in goodness, and make every coming year better for your Deen and your dunya. Ameen ya Rabbal Alameen.

— From your mad princess
